## 5. Local voice and internet connection

The narration engine generates speech on the device. The first time you use a voice, Folio requests confirmation before downloading the Supertonic voice package from the public Sherpa-ONNX project repository on GitHub. This download requires internet access, uses device storage, and may be cancelled.

After it is installed, speech generation is local. This does not make the websites you open offline: web-page reading remains dependent on the connection and rules of the website you choose.
