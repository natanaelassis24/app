Local voice, internet, and background playback

The Supertonic voice package is downloaded only after your confirmation from the public Sherpa-ONNX repository on GitHub. Once installed, speech is generated on your device. Reading websites still depends on your connection and the rules of the website you open.

When narration is active, Folio may display controls in the notification and on the lock screen. The system can interrupt playback according to battery, memory, calls, and device settings.

Privacy Policy

Folio has no user account, advertising, analytics, or dedicated server that receives the text being read. It still keeps some information locally to provide its features.

Data stored on the device

  • theme, language, speed, and voice state;
  • up to four recently opened sites and the reading position;
  • the PDF you choose, processed locally, and a possible Android read permission for that document;
  • temporary narration segments in private cache while playback is prepared;
  • the optionally downloaded voice package in private storage;
  • cache, first-party cookies, and web storage from sites visited through WebView.

Connections and third parties

When you open a website, search, or download a voice, your device connects directly to the selected service. Those services may receive normal technical connection data, such as IP address and headers, and are governed by their own policies. Folio uses HTTPS only and blocks third-party cookies, mixed content, and geolocation.

How to delete data

To delete preferences, recent sites, reading position, cache, and the local voice, go to Android Settings → Apps → Folio → Storage → Clear data. Uninstalling the app also removes its private data. This does not delete records kept by external websites or the voice-download provider.
